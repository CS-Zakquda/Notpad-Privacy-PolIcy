Privacy Policy for Notepad Anywhere

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Notepad Anywhere ("the extension") is a note-taking browser extension. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

What data is stored

The extension stores only what you type into it: note titles, note content, and basic preferences (such as your chosen theme). This data is saved using Chrome's built-in storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local) and syncs automatically across browsers where you are signed into the same Google/Chrome account, exactly as Chrome handles bookmarks or browser settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes your notes.
  • We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.

Permissions

The extension requests only the storage permission, used solely to save your notes locally and sync them via Chrome's own infrastructure.

Data deletion

Deleting a note moves it to the extension's Trash, where it can be permanently deleted at any time (or is automatically purged after 30 days). Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored data; synced data is removed from your Google account's Chrome sync storage according to Google's own sync data retention practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
